How to Choose a Tablet: 3 Key Factors

1. Size and Portability

10-inch is ideal for media and reading. 11-13 inch works better for drawing and work tasks.

For note-taking or digital art, Apple Pencil support on iPads is unmatched.

3. Budget

Under $300 is fine for streaming and reading. Serious work tasks benefit from $500+ models.
